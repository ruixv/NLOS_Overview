Core Reading Path

Start with the field-defining Nature papers, then follow the experimental, algorithmic, learning and modality-expansion trajectory.

25 October 2026 · physically informed neural transient fields: Xu et al. (ICCPA 2026 / SPIE 14296) update neural transient-field reconstruction with physical constraints and efficient hash coordinate encoding, extending the NeTF family alongside neural implicit surfaces and differentiable transient rendering.

25 October 2026 · frequency-aware single-pixel NLOS: Yang et al. (Journal of Electronic Imaging 2026) combine learned binary illumination codes with frequency-aware reconstruction to recover hidden scenes from intensity-only single-pixel measurements under strong relay-wall blur and low sampling. This extends the fixed-pattern coding lineage toward optical-computational co-design.

24 October 2026 · dynamic-relay milestone backfill: La Manna et al. (Optics Express 2020) reconstruct hidden scenes while a non-planar relay surface moves, using separate time-resolved measurements to associate changing relay positions with hidden-scene photon returns. This hardware/calibration precursor connects static-wall transient NLOS to later non-planar 3D-RSD and arbitrary-relay GPU inversion. The paper is now integrated into the historical timeline, explorer, and active NLOS survey section.

28 October 2026 · radar multipath citation trace: Lin et al. (IEEE TGRS 2024) use UAV-borne array-InSAR, fitted building planes and multipath reachability to relocate virtual NLOS targets to their true 3D positions in urban canyons, reporting sub-0.5 m errors and expanded ground-range and 3D coverage. This airborne SAR reconstruction path complements mobile mmWave scene reconstruction and target-centric corner radar imaging.

28 October 2026 · distributed passive ISAC sensing: Mursia et al. (IEEE ISAC 2026, accepted; to appear) use distributed wireless channel measurements and hyperdimensional computing for risk-aware classification of passive people and movable obstacles beyond a robot’s field of view. The paper reports up to 89% accuracy under severe occlusion; it broadens NLOS sensing toward infrastructure-assisted robotics but does not reconstruct hidden images or 3D shape.

28 October 2026 · diffraction-based NLOS boundary reconstruction: Chen, Xiong, and Xu (arXiv:2610.31271) implement a constructive inverse-scattering method that uses diffracted arrival times to locate polygon corners and amplitudes to infer corner angles and nearby shadowed edges. The evidence is synthetic 2D wave simulation, so this is a mathematical diffraction bridge rather than a measured relay-wall 3D imager; no final venue was verified.

28 October 2026 · inverse-diffraction theory in 2D: Bao, Chen, Lu, and Xiong (arXiv:2607.22174) prove unique recovery of visible polygonal-obstacle geometry from a single pulse and diffracted-wave measurements on a receiver curve, without relying on reflected paths. The work is a mathematical inverse-scattering result, not a measured NLOS imaging system; no final venue was verified.

28 October 2026 · inverse diffraction for 3D cones: Bao et al. (arXiv:2608.04675) extend the theory to strictly convex conical obstacles in three dimensions, using diffraction arrival times/directions and coefficients measured on a small receiver patch in the reflection shadow. This establishes a 3D theoretical counterpart to the 2D polygon theory; final venue remains unverified.

28 October 2026 · RIS-assisted MIMO NLOS angular sensing: Dai, Chen, and Jin (IEEE SAM 2026) combine a reconfigurable intelligent surface with a compressed electromagnetic-fluid vector-antenna MIMO radar. PARAFAC, ESPRIT, and vector-cross-product processing pair multi-target departure/arrival angles and polarization; the published results are simulation-based target localization, not dense 3D reconstruction.

24 October 2026 · historical venue and RF-lineage audit: PathFinder (IEEE/RSJ IROS 2024) demonstrates moving-camera passive NLOS tracking; GeRaF (NeurIPS 2025) introduces physics-based implicit RF geometry and precedes its GeRaF 2.0 (CVPR 2026) extension. Both studies are now cited in the survey with final-venue metadata.

24 October 2026 · six citation-traced research gaps: Ding et al. (TPAMI 2024) introduce curvature priors for under-sampled transient inversion; Yang and Isogawa (ICIP 2024) enhance circular transient sinograms; Wang et al. (SPIE 2024) investigate range/resolution; Wang et al. (SPIE 2025) investigate Lissajous scans; Wen et al. (TGRS 2024) recover detail from sparse NLOS ISAR; and Wen et al. (TVT 2025) reconstruct hidden moving targets with CMTI. Survey text, citations, and the regenerated 70-page PDF have been verified together (24 October 2026).

23 October 2026 · RF modality backfill: Gajewski and Łopatka (Sensors 2026) fuse circular-array multipath DOA estimation, backward ray tracing and CNN sector filtering for measured single-site urban NLOS emitter localization. This is RF sensing rather than 3D hidden-scene reconstruction; sector errors create large outliers. Survey source integrated; PDF rebuilt and validated on 24 October 2026.

23 October 2026 · LCT forward-citation backfill: Zhou et al. (Applied Physics Letters 2023) suppress classical backprojection artifacts with absorption of low-confidence voxels; Fang et al. (Acta Optica Sinica 2023) calibrate non-Lambertian relay scattering with a genetic algorithm and recover commodity-ToF depth using Lucy–Richardson deconvolution. Both are integrated in the survey source; PDF rebuilt and validated on 24 October 2026.

22 October 2026 · forward-citation backfill: Balaji et al. (Optics Express 2023) formalize coherent indirect-imaging correlography and its pose-dependent resolution; Li et al. (Optics Express 2023) isolate weak NLOS target echoes using correlation without indiscriminate photon accumulation; Zhang et al. (Optics Communications 2023) establish fixed Archimedean-spiral relay-wall scanning, a direct precursor to the 2026 adaptive spiral method. All three are integrated in the survey source; PDF rebuilt and validated on 24 October 2026.

19 October 2026 · LCT successor: Wang et al., Measurement-Adaptive Light-Cone Transform (MALCT) adapt the confocal LCT Fourier inverse through measurement-conditioned learned responses and frequency-dependent regularization, evaluated on synthetic and measured transients including 32×32 spatial sampling. arXiv preprint (16 October 2026); final venue not verified. Survey PDF rebuilt and validated on 28 October 2026 (72 pages; Actions run 40654782621).

21 October 2026 · Acoustic NLOS multi-object sensing: Çalışan, Applied Sciences 16(20), 10293 (17 October 2026) distinguishes four paired-object combinations from wall-mediated secondary acoustic echoes using STFT features and repeated recording-level validation (120 recordings; 98.5% mean random-forest accuracy). This is semantic recognition, not hidden 3D reconstruction; transfer to new rooms/sessions is unverified. Survey source integrated; PDF rebuilt and validated on 24 October 2026.

21 October 2026 · PRCV 2023 citation backfill: Chang et al. (PRCV 2023) jointly optimize compressed active NLOS measurements and reconstruction, while DFAR-Net (PRCV 2023) fuses polarized infrared intensity and degree-of-polarization features for passive hidden-scene recovery. Both are Springer LNCS 14430 conference papers, pp. 28–40 and 41–52 respectively. The survey source is updated; PDF rebuild remains pending.

20 October 2026 · citation-traced RF NLOS relays: Metasight (ACM SenSys 2023) encodes hidden-target angles using compact passive mmWave metasurfaces; its later citing work Now You See It! (accepted ACM MobiCom 2026 demo) demonstrates programmable 28-GHz RIS steering and calibrated differential detection of a static passive target. Both are NLOS radar sensing, not dense 3D imaging; the demo proceedings are forthcoming. Survey PDF rebuilt and validated on 28 October 2026 (72 pages; Actions run 40654782621).

October 2026 modality reconciliation: Yan et al. (IEEE TAES 2026) cover RIS-aided radar detection; Sanjeeva Reddy and Veera Reddy (2026) derive bistatic IRS estimation bounds; Yasmeen et al. (2026) characterize quantized RIS scattering and non-specular micro-Doppler sensing; Wang et al. (2026) review acoustic NLOS localization. These studies concern adjacent sensing rather than dense 3D reconstruction. Literature sources updated on 28 October 2026; the survey covers the Doppler-aided mmWave position--velocity tracking preprint, hybrid active--passive color reconstruction, and the accepted IEEE ISAC 2026 distributed-channel/HDC study of passive object sensing beyond a robot’s field of view.

Newly integrated historical and transient-imaging links: consumer-camera periscopy (SPIE 2019) and door-frame 3D passive imaging (IEEE RAPID 2022) precede the mature TERI system; temporal-decoupling NLOS super-resolution (Optics and Lasers in Engineering 2026) extends the transient reconstruction branch. Miao et al. (Optics Express 2023, temporal encoding) recovers finer transients from coarse detector timing; Saunders et al. (IEEE ICIP 2021, ERTI optimization) analyzes edge-resolved acquisition. PDF rebuilt and validated on 24 October 2026.

Coherent synthetic-wavelength milestone: Willomitzer et al. (Nature Communications 2021) recover hidden geometry with high resolution and wide field of view using optical-interference-derived synthetic wavelengths, complementing transient ToF and phasor-field systems.

Optical hidden-physiology frontier: Chen et al. (Optica 13(9), 2026) extend indirect optical NLOS sensing to hidden vital signs; this is physiological sensing rather than dense 3D scene reconstruction.

Passive computational-periscopy and thermal precursors: Doorway Camera (ICCP 2022) and occlusion-aided 3D NLOS (RAPID 2024) explore aperture coding without transient capture; Hashemi et al. (ICCP 2023) exploit thermal parallax to recover weak hidden-person signals.

Active–passive color/geometry fusion: Shi et al. (Photonics Research 2026, accepted 14 October) jointly recover hidden 3D geometry and color by decomposing active and passive measurements into complementary amplitude and color information; available as an early-posting article pending final issue assignment.

Passive learned reconstruction and display-side-channel branch: Zhou et al. (Photonics 2026) recover bounded hidden-screen structure from a single diffuse-wall RGB image using survivability-guided feature fusion. The reported results are restricted to controlled imaging conditions.

Passive aperture and multi-object reconstruction lineage: Shi et al. (Optics Express 2024) extends computational-periscopy-like geometry from edge occluders to large transmissive windows, using depth separation to reconstruct multiple overlapping hidden objects with an ordinary camera.

Polarization and multispectral passive NLOS: PI-NLOS (Optics Express 2023) fuses polarized LWIR intensity and degree-of-polarization cues; multispectral deep fusion (Science China Information Sciences 2025) combines visible, SWIR and LWIR observations for hidden-person reconstruction.

2025 citation-lineage backfill: Liu and Li (IEEE ICCEA 2025) use physics-guided dual-branch attention for active NLOS reconstruction; SG-NLOSTrack (IEEE ITSC 2025) uses camera semantics, 4D radar reflections and Kalman filtering for hidden-target tracking rather than full 3D imaging.

Newly verified reconstruction and acquisition directions: TriNLOS (ECCV 2026) uses triplane neural volumes after learned LCT; NLOSdiffuser (ICME 2025) combines steady-state inversion with diffusion priors; and gradient-gated SPAD (ESSERC 2025) advances transient acquisition hardware.

Photon-noise and forward-model backfill: Tu et al. (Laser & Optoelectronics Progress 2023) trained an attention-enhanced denoiser on Monte Carlo-simulated single-photon NLOS data and validated it on a physical system; Scholes and Leach (Sensors 2024) modeled dToF-SPAD transient photon statistics, including occlusion, reflectance and secondary scattering. The latter is simulation infrastructure, not a reconstruction method.

Classical transient inverse-method backfill: Jin et al. (Optics Express 2018) separated overlapping hidden-object ellipsoid modes; their Optics Letters 2018 follow-up deconvolved transient histograms using visible-scene backscatter priors. Shi et al. (Applied Optics 2020) sharpened edges by re-projection, while Feng and Gao (Optics Letters 2020) suppressed streaks and shot noise through aperture/coherence weighting. The subsequent multi-light-field learned long-range NLOS approach (Photonics 2023) demonstrates a separate data-driven route to hidden-target reconstruction at sensor-to-relay distances above 50 m.

Earlier acquisition and hardware lineage: foveated NLOS (COSI 2020) proposed transient-plus-correlography capture; picosecond-gated NLOS (Optics Express 2021) demonstrated photon-efficient hidden-target and vibration sensing; fractal SNSPD NLOS (Optics Express 2023) demonstrated 1560/1997 nm reconstruction. The first is a conference digest; the latter two are journal papers.

Fast coherent NLOS acquisition: Huang et al. (PRL 2024) established comb-calibrated FMCW hidden-scene imaging and vibrometry; Qian et al. (SPIE Photonics Europe 2026) demonstrated predistorted near-infrared FMCW LiDAR with approximately 0.2 s acquisition per NLOS frame. The latter is a conference demonstration, not a journal result.

Adjacent NLOS sensing and localization (not full hidden-scene imaging): PRISM (MobiHoc 2025) uses RIS frequency shifts for mmWave target localization; Mao et al. (IWS 2026) recover hidden vital signs; Tian et al. (2024) use equivalent-source time reversal for hidden acoustic-source localization; and Sroka et al. (JASA 2026) incorporate rigid-boundary acoustics. Earlier acoustic source-localization milestones (distinct from hidden-scene imaging) include Mak & Furukawa (AIM 2009), Singh et al. (JASA 2012), Kitić et al. (ICASSP 2014), Takami et al. (IROS 2016), and An et al. (ICRA 2019). Chakraborty et al. (SPAWC 2025) and King et al. (Applied Acoustics 2021) expand radar and acoustic NLOS localization. Explore the full RF and acoustic branches in the Paper Explorer.

Verified RF/multipath developments: Chen et al. (IEEE TRS 2025) jointly localize LOS and NLOS targets under clutter; Zhang et al. (IEEE TWC 2026) recover double-bounce radio-SLAM landmarks; and Tong et al. (IEEE TWC 2026) couple distributed-MIMO NLOS imaging with over-the-air synchronization. These are RF mapping/localization advances rather than dense hidden-object reconstruction.
