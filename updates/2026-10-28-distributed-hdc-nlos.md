## Candidate verified

**Leveraging Distributed Sensing and Hyperdimensional Computing for Non-Line-of-Sight Passive Object Sensing** — Placido Mursia, Marco Rossanese, Francesco Devoti, Adrian Lendinez Ibanez, Lanfranco Zanzi, Xi Li, and Xavier Costa-Perez.

- **Final venue:** IEEE Conference on Integrated Sensing and Communications (ISAC 2026), accepted and to appear; conference dates are 16–18 November 2026.
- **Source/preprint:** [arXiv:2610.37519](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.37519), submitted 27 October 2026. The arXiv record explicitly says the paper is to appear in the ISAC 2026 proceedings, so the repository labels the conference as the venue and retains arXiv as the accessible paper link.
- **Contribution:** Distributed wireless channel measurements are embedded with hyperdimensional computing and used for risk-aware classification of passive people and movable obstacles outside a robot’s direct field of view. The paper reports up to 89% accuracy under severe occlusion in ray-tracing simulations and ROS-based smart-factory validation.
- **Scope decision:** Relevant adjacent NLOS sensing/robotics, but not an image- or shape-reconstruction method. The README, homepage, and Paper Explorer explicitly preserve this distinction.
