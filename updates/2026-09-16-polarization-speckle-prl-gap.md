# 2026-09-16 update: polarization-speckle single-pixel NLOS

## Verified missing paper

Yijun Zhou, Wenwen Li, Wei Li, Xin Huang, Chen Dai, Zhong-Pei Xiao, Zheng-Ping Li, Feihu Xu, and Jian-Wei Pan, "Non-Line-of-Sight Single-Pixel Imaging Using Polarization Speckle Modulation," Physical Review Letters 136(14), 143801 (2026). DOI: 10.1103/kd8v-fykm. Published 6 April 2026.

## Why it belongs

This is a direct optical NLOS imaging paper, not a tangential citation. It introduces polarization as an additional illumination/modulation degree of freedom for scanning-free NLOS single-pixel imaging. Changing incident polarization on a rough relay wall produces diverse polarization-speckle patterns that encode the hidden scene; a single-pixel detector records the return. Experiments demonstrate high-fidelity, millimeter-scale hidden-object imaging through a keyhole, and the paper introduces non-invasive calibration based on the angular memory effect of polarization speckles.
