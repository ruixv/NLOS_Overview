# 14 October 2026 — IET joint LOS/NLOS mmWave radar

Su, Shuohan; Gong, Pengcheng; Li, Qixing; Liu, Jian; Zhao, Yuanjun; Zeng, Xiangjin; Wu, Yuntao. "Joint Localisation of Line-of-Sight and Non-Line-of-Sight Targets via Deep Learning-Assisted Millimetre-Wave Radar." *IET Radar, Sonar & Navigation* 20(1), e70242 (2026). First published 22 September 2026. DOI https://doi.org/10.1049/rsn2.70242.

This is joint LOS/NLOS **target localization**, not dense 3D hidden-scene imaging. Geometry-partitioned urban T-junction radar, region-weighted range–angle–Doppler sparse recovery, IAA-Net/RIRM-Net unfolding, and measured L-/T-junction tests distinguish weak hidden targets from strong LOS returns.
